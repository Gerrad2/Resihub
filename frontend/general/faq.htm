Frequently Asked Questions

How Can We Help?

Find answers to the most common questions about ResiHub, student accommodation, bookings, landlords and payments.

ResiHub is a platform for student accommodation in Nigeria. Students can search for hostels, self-contained apartments and shared rooms near their university, and landlords can list their properties for students to find.

Go to Browse Rooms and choose your university, or search by location. You can narrow the results by price range, room type, availability and amenities such as Wi-Fi, water, electricity and security.

Create a landlord account, then add a listing. Include the title, price, property type, area, university, amenities and clear photos. Your listing appears to students once it is published.

A Verified label means the listing and its landlord have been checked by the ResiHub team. Students can still review every listing carefully, but verified listings are the safest place to start.

Yes. From your dashboard you can edit the details, update photos and prices, and change the availability to available, unavailable or booked. Unavailable listings are hidden from students' results.

Booking requests appear in your dashboard as pending. Review the student's request, then approve or reject it. The student is notified as soon as you respond.

Open the room and choose Book Now to request an inspection. Pick a date and time that works for you. The landlord will approve or reject the request, and you will be notified of the outcome.

A request stays pending until the landlord responds. Its status changes to approved, rejected or completed, and you will receive a notification each time it changes.

The price and payment steps are shown on the listing before you commit. Always check the total amount, what it covers and when it is due before you pay.

Be careful. Never send money before you have seen the room, spoken to the landlord and confirmed the booking through ResiHub. If anyone asks you to pay outside the platform or pressures you to pay quickly, contact our support team.

Refunds follow the terms shown on the listing before you pay. If you have a payment problem, contact our support team with your booking details and we will help you resolve it.

ResiHub does not ask for your full card details on this site. Keep your sign-in details private, and report any suspicious message or request to our support team straight away.
