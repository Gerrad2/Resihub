Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run ResiHub. This includes:

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and sign-in details. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored as plain text.
  • Profile details: for students, your university, faculty, department, matric number, level, state and address. For landlords, your business name and CAC number. Date of birth, gender and emergency contact details are collected only if you choose to add them.
  • Listing and booking details: property information, photos, inspection requests, saved rooms and reviews you submit.
  • Messages: messages you send through ResiHub to landlords or to us.
  • Usage information: basic device and browser details, and how you use the site, so we can keep it working and fix problems.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • show listings and help students find rooms near their university;
  • process inspection requests and send notifications about their status;
  • deliver messages and provide customer support;
  • keep the platform secure, prevent fraud and block double bookings;
  • improve ResiHub;
  • send email updates you have chosen to receive. Marketing emails are off unless you turn them on.

Cookies

ResiHub uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your settings. Without them, parts of the site will not work. You can clear cookies in your browser at any time, but you may need to sign in again.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • With landlords: when you request an inspection, the landlord receives the details needed to arrange it, such as your name and the message you send.
  • With service providers: companies that host and run the platform for us, and only to the extent they need to do that work.
  • When required by law: if we are legally required to disclose information, or to protect people's safety and rights.

Security

We use secure connections and restrict access to personal information to the people and systems that need it. Each user can only see their own private records. No system is completely secure, so keep your password private and tell us straight away if you notice anything suspicious.

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your account and your personal information, except where we must keep some records by law;
  • stop sending you marketing emails at any time.

You can update most details from your profile. For anything else, email us at the address below.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at privacy@resihub.com.
